About Me

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

My research focuses on scientific machine learning, inverse problems, and computational imaging.

I am advised by Qin Li and Leonardo Andrés Zepeda Núñez, and I am a member of Kinetics at Madison.

I received my B.A. in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from UC Berkeley in 2020, where I was mentored by Rainer Sachs.

Beginning in Fall 2026, I will join the School of Mathematics at the Georgia Institute of Technology as a Visiting Assistant Professor, where I will work with Wei Zhu.
