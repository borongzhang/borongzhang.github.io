About Me

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the School of Mathematics at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where I work with Wei Zhu.

My research focuses on scientific machine learning, inverse problems, and computational imaging.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2026, where I was advised by Qin Li and Leonardo Andrés Zepeda Núñez, and was a member of Kinetics at Madison.

I received my B.A. in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from UC Berkeley in 2020, where I was mentored by Rainer Sachs.
